Spartanburg Academic Movement

Collaborating across cross-functional teams to develop and execute strategic content calendars for Big Mo and Hello Family, groups that help Spartanburg County students. My role involves translating organizational goals into engaging visual stories: designing custom graphics, producing high-impact Reels, and writing captions. I manage posting for Instagram and Facebook through Sprout Social while contributing to creative brainstorming and long-term campaign strategy.
